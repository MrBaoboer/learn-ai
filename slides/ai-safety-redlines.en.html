AI Safety Red Lines: Four Non-Negotiable Principles

Four absolute non-negotiables when your enterprise uses AI. Remember these four, and you avoid 90% of AI security incidents. Click any red-line card to see details. Violating any one of them = a security incident.

Four Red Lines
Red Line #1
Sensitive Data Stays Inside
Customer data and internal documents must not enter external AI
Red Line #2
Credentials Never Enter Conversations
Passwords, API Keys, and Tokens must never be pasted into AI
Red Line #3
High-Risk Actions Require Human Confirmation
Dropping databases, transferring funds, and changing permissions — AI cannot act alone
Compliance Baseline
Approve Before Use · Label After
New tools need approval, published content needs AI labeling, no resource abuse
