Context Management

Compression Is the Art of Trade-offs

What to do when the context is too long? Neither deleting everything nor keeping everything is right: some can be deleted (old tool outputs), some cannot be deleted (user's exact words), and some need paid compression (full summary). Product managers need a decision framework.

Compression is not about deleting everything or keeping everything—it's about finding balance between information value and token cost. A good compression strategy is like an experienced editor: knowing what is filler that can be cut, and what is the user's exact words that must be kept.
