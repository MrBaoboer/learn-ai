Recap · nine lessons in three lines
Spot

Lessons 1–5: four handles: are the three states complete? Are dangerous actions reversible? Can the flow lose another step? Did anyone invent odd interactions? To judge usefulness, click in that order.

Detail

Lessons 6–7: UI parts: mutually exclusive radio buttons, multi-select checkboxes, instant toggles, dropdown only past one screen; button verbs name the consequence; labels speak the user's language.

Translate

Lessons 8–9: write it for AI: brief with persona + goal + scenario; the spec lists all five states, nails edge cases, spells out error prevention—together they're the full prompt.
