Why the page numbers aren’t in numeric order

The path is ordered by dependency. Asking sits first, because every later step takes its output as input: it breaks the material, it writes the questions, it helps you write the notes. Skip this layer and every later move is working on answers that already drifted.

Guarding against hallucination follows asking — it decides whether this sentence stays. The keep-it group sits after breaking material: you need something understood before you have something to keep. Self-check comes after keeping, because it checks whether it stayed. Path-planning is last: it fills the week’s calendar with every earlier move.

Point at This Page and Ask AI is pinned at the start — you need it whenever a later page stalls. Let AI Quiz Me Right Now is pinned at the end, so after the path she quizzes you by title.
