Action List · Three Things to Do This Week
1

Calculate Your Runway

Open your accounting app, calculate "savings ÷ monthly expenses", write that number of months on a sticky note and attach it to your screen. Below 12, this number becomes the first constraint on all your decisions going forward.

2

Write a One-Sentence Business

In one sentence, explain what you do, who you sell to, and why it's you — send it to three friends. If anyone can't repeat it back, keep revising until they can. A business you can't explain clearly also can't raise money.

3

Add an Exit Clause

Pull out any ongoing partnership (outsourcing, revenue-sharing, co-development) and add one page: how is an early exit calculated, who owns the output, when does the split start counting. Write it while the relationship is good.

Closing Thoughts

What AI changes is how much one person can do — it doesn't change the logic of business. Think big on direction, be relentless on product, watch your cash, negotiate partnerships clearly. Every word Lei Jun said to startups of dozens applies equally to a company of one. The stronger the tools, the more valuable the fundamentals. The rest: focus, excellence, reputation, speed.
