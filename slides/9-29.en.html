Why Observability Matters
For Developers
  • Pinpoint exactly which step the Agent failed at
  • Identify useless loops and wasted tokens
  • Optimize the tool call chain
For Product Managers
  • Understand actual user journeys
  • Quantify the cost of each feature
  • Provide data to drive the next iteration
Without looking at logs, you'll never know what went wrong with your Agent. Observability is the foundation of production AI. An Agent without a dashboard is like a car without an instrument panel: you don't know the fuel level, the RPMs, or when it's going to break down.
